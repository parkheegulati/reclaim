How it works

Three steps. Full recovery.

From detection to rupees — completely automated.

01

Detect & Classify

Ingests failed payments from Razorpay. Classifies every failure by type — transient errors, soft declines, hard blocks. Risk-scored and prioritised by recovery value.

TRANSIENT_FAILURE SOFT_DECLINE HARD_DECLINE
→
02

AI Diagnose & Decide

Gemini AI analyses root cause and customer sentiment. Selects the optimal action — auto-retry, payment link, EMI, or escalate — with confidence score and full reasoning.

AUTO_RETRY PAYMENT_LINK ESCALATE
→
03

Recover & Audit

Executes bounded workflow — retries with idempotency, generates Razorpay payment links, sends Hinglish messages, logs every action with timestamp, reasoning, and outcome.
